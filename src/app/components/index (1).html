⚠️ Critical Acclimatization Period
This 5-hour rest is essential for your health and safety at 11,500 ft altitude. Avoid any physical activity, stay hydrated with 3-4 liters of water, and listen to your body. Symptoms like headache or dizziness are normal but inform hotel staff if they persist.
💡 Acclimatization Tips
Drink 3-4 liters of water throughout the day
Take deep, slow breaths if you feel breathless
Avoid alcohol and heavy meals
Keep room well-ventilated
Take short naps if tired
